# ✨ SKINORA

> ## **Your Personal Wellness, Self-Care & Style Companion**

SKINORA is an AI-powered **personal wellness, self-care, health education, and styling platform** designed to bring multiple aspects of everyday well-being into one personalized digital experience.

The platform combines **skincare guidance, health education, menstrual wellness, lifestyle tracking, myth-busting, habit-support programs, computer vision, machine learning, face-shape analysis, facial-feature analysis, and personalized style recommendations**.

SKINORA provides two personalized experiences through dedicated **Male and Female dashboards**, allowing users to access features relevant to their preferences and needs.

> **SKINORA is designed for education, awareness, lifestyle support, and personalization. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.**

---

# 📌 Project Description

SKINORA is an AI-powered personal wellness and self-care platform that combines health awareness, skincare, lifestyle management, menstrual wellness, personal styling, and machine learning.

The platform helps users:

- Understand their skin and personal-care needs
- Learn about healthy lifestyle practices
- Track menstrual cycles
- Learn about PCOD/PCOS through educational resources
- Separate health myths from evidence-based information
- Explore health-related Dos & Don'ts
- Build healthier habits
- Access support-oriented plans for reducing harmful habits
- Analyze face shape and facial features
- Discover personalized hairstyles and styling suggestions
- Explore clothing and colour recommendations
- Track personal progress through interactive dashboards

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

People often use multiple disconnected platforms for:

- Skincare
- Health information
- Menstrual tracking
- Lifestyle management
- Personal styling
- Face-shape analysis
- Habit management
- Health awareness

This creates fragmented experiences and makes it difficult to maintain a consistent personal wellness routine.

Additionally, online health and beauty information can contain myths, misinformation, unrealistic expectations, or generalized recommendations that may not apply to everyone.

**SKINORA aims to bring these experiences together into one personalized platform while encouraging informed decision-making and responsible self-care.**

🔐 Privacy & Responsible AI

SKINORA handles potentially sensitive personal information, so privacy and responsible AI should be core design principles.

Recommended practices include:

• Obtain user consent before collecting personal information.
• Minimize unnecessary data collection.
• Avoid storing images unless required.
• Protect user data.
• Secure API endpoints.
• Encrypt sensitive information where appropriate.
• Never expose authentication credentials or API keys.
• Allow users to delete their data.
• Clearly explain how AI recommendations work.
• Avoid making medical diagnoses.
• Avoid presenting recommendations as guaranteed medical outcomes.
• Encourage professional healthcare consultation when appropriate.


⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

SKINORA is an educational and lifestyle-support platform.

It is not a medical device and should not be used to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions.

Health, menstrual, PCOD/PCOS, skincare, nutrition, and habit-support information should be presented as general educational guidance.

For symptoms, diagnosis, treatment decisions, addiction concerns, or other health issues, users should consult qualified healthcare professionals.
